Current Simulated Workflow

Fragmented Multi-Register Entry

ASHA visits 1 household and writes the same name, age, and pregnancy details into 3 separate paper registers and disconnected portals.

  • 1. Maternal ANC Register: Writes Mother name, age, gestation, LMP date, high risk notes.
  • 2. Child Immunisation Register: Re-enters Mother name, child details, vaccines, next due dates.
  • 3. Village Census Survey: Re-enters Household ID, family head, pregnancy status, child count.
  • 4. Data Contradictions: Discrepancies in spelling, dates, or age between physical books.
